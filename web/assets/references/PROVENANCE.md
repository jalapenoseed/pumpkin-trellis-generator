# Pumpkin reference assets — 9 October 2026

Both PNGs were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. They are synthetic photographic-style images, not photographs or scans. No third-party photograph was used as an input. The board is a visual guide; its botanical captions are not independently verified. The rind image is used as the workshop's color map; the normal and roughness maps are still procedural and are not measured PBR captures. Exact seamlessness is not certified.

## pumpkin-form-surface.png

The full-size reference board contains six fruit forms and six close-up material studies. Accessible in the workshop under **Surface reference**.

Prompt:

Create a very high resolution photorealistic botanical material reference board for a realistic procedural pumpkin game asset. Landscape 3:2 with twelve clean photographic panels, minimal fine labels only. Six upper panels: actual-looking mature orange round pumpkin whole front with irregular deep ribs and dry stem; tall orange pumpkin side; squat russet heirloom pumpkin; ivory pumpkin; dark muted green ribbed squash; tan warty pumpkin. Six lower macro panels: orange rind with extremely fine pores, diffuse freckles and gentle waxy sheen, pale scars along rib crests; stem attachment recessed corky woody socket; dry five-ridged curling stem with fibrous snapped end; green five-lobed pumpkin leaf upper surface with radiating branching veins and hair; leaf underside with paler veins and fine hairs; living hairy runner, tightly coiled tendril and yellow five pointed squash blossom. Neutral warm grey backgrounds, cross-polarized soft diffuse photographic illumination with just enough form shading, true scale botanical macro photography, muted natural pigment, no toy plastic, no stylization, no dramatic cinematic lighting. Rich realism reference for model shape and material construction, not a game screenshot. Title 'PUMPKIN • FORM & SURFACE'.

## pumpkin-rind-albedo.png

The workshop repeats this neutral rind color map around the fruit and tints it for orange, ivory and green cultivars.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square diffuse albedo texture for a 3D pumpkin surface, full image usable as a texture, no border, no text. Extremely detailed close-up of REAL ivory pumpkin rind, orthographic flat scan of skin only, neutral warm off-white and pale grey color range, desaturated. Micro pores, very fine organic stippling, sparse tiny grey-tan freckles, slightly elongated longitudinal scuffs, patches of natural wax bloom, a few very thin dry cork scars, subtle translucent mottling of real vegetable skin. No recognizable whole pumpkin, no large rib shapes, no stem, no leaf, no background. Even cross-polarized illumination: NO highlights, NO directional shading, NO shadows, no ambient occlusion baked in. Left and right edges and top and bottom must tile seamlessly. Fine scale skin grain with very restrained natural imperfections, NOT reptile scales, not stone, not leather, not fabric. Bright neutral ivory diffuse map that will be tinted orange, green or cream by the renderer. Fill entire square with surface texture at very high photographic fidelity.
